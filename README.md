# SCREENED.

### Your screen time in perspective.

**SCREENED.** is a simple web-based calculator that shows how much of your life could be spent looking at screens based on your daily screen time and assumed lifespan.

The idea is simple:

> **Hours don't feel like years until you add them up.**

## 🌐 Live Website

**[Visit SCREENED.](https://screened-your-screen-time.vercel.app/)**

## ✨ Features

* Calculate lifetime screen time
* Enter daily screen time in hours and minutes
* Quick-select common screen-time values
* Adjustable daily screen-time slider
* Choose an assumed lifespan from 70 to 90 years
* See projected screen time in:

  * Years
  * Days
  * Percentage of each day
* Simple and minimal interface
* Fully responsive design for desktop and mobile
* No account required
* No data is saved

## 🧮 How It Works

SCREENED. takes your average daily screen time and projects it across your assumed lifetime.

For example, if you spend **3 hours every day** on screens and assume an **80-year lifespan**, the calculator estimates how many years of your life could be spent looking at screens.

The goal isn't to tell you what to do.

It's simply to make the number visible.

## 🎯 Why SCREENED.?

Most people know they spend hours on their phones and computers.

But **3 hours today** doesn't feel like much.

Multiply that by years, and the number becomes much harder to ignore.

SCREENED. exists to answer one simple question:

**"What does my screen time actually cost me over a lifetime?"**

## 🔒 Privacy

SCREENED. does not require an account and does not save your calculations.
